Poly Weather

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 September 2026

Overview

Poly Weather is designed to provide weather information without requiring an account. The app does not sell personal information.

Information stored on your device

Poly Weather stores your most recently selected city and the most recently retrieved weather forecast on your device. This allows the app to restore your selected location and display the most recently saved forecast when a network connection is unavailable.

Weather and location searches

When you search for a city or refresh weather information, the app sends the selected city coordinates or search text to Open-Meteo in order to retrieve weather and geocoding results.

Third-party service

Poly Weather uses Open-Meteo to provide weather and geocoding data. Open-Meteo may process technical information necessary to provide its service, such as network information. Please review Open-Meteo's policies for details of its data practices.

Analytics and advertising

Poly Weather does not include third-party advertising or analytics SDKs.
